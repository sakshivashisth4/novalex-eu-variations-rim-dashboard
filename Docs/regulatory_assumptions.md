\# Regulatory Assumptions

\#\# Purpose

This document explains the regulatory assumptions used in the Novalexib EU Variations RIM Dashboard project.

The data is fictional and created for portfolio demonstration purposes. However, the project structure is based on realistic regulatory affairs concepts used in EU post-authorisation lifecycle management.

\#\# Product Assumptions

| Field | Assumption |  
|---|---|  
| Product Name | Novalexib |  
| Product Type | Fictional medicinal product |  
| Therapeutic Area | Oncology |  
| Region | European Union |  
| Health Authority | EMA |  
| Procedure Type | Centralised Procedure |  
| MAA Approval Date | 15-Mar-2022 |  
| Lifecycle Stage | Post-authorisation lifecycle management |  
| First Variation Submission | 15-Jun-2024 |

\#\# Lifecycle Assumptions

Novalexib is assumed to have completed development and initial EU approval before the start of the simulated variation portfolio.

The lifecycle is divided into:

1\. Development and CMC preparation  
2\. EU MAA submission and assessment  
3\. European Commission approval  
4\. EU launch readiness  
5\. Post-authorisation variation management  
6\. Agency query management  
7\. Publishing and archive tracking  
8\. Long-term lifecycle maintenance

\#\# Variation Assumptions

The project includes simulated EU variation categories:

\- Type IA  
\- Type IAIN  
\- Type IB  
\- Type II  
\- Extension-type submissions where applicable

The variation categories are used to represent different levels of regulatory complexity.

\#\# Risk Assumptions

Risk level is assigned based on the likely regulatory impact of the variation.

| Risk Level | Meaning |  
|---|---|  
| Low | Administrative or minor update with limited regulatory impact |  
| Medium | Moderate quality, labelling, or procedural impact |  
| High | Major CMC, clinical, safety, or implementation impact |

Type II variations are assumed to carry the highest regulatory burden because they often require prior approval and detailed supporting documentation.

\#\# Submission Status Assumptions

The project uses the following submission statuses:

| Status | Meaning |  
|---|---|  
| Not Started | Submission has been identified but preparation has not begun |  
| In Preparation | Submission is being prepared internally |  
| Under Assessment | Submission has been sent to the health authority and is under review |  
| Approved \- Pending Implementation | Approval received but implementation is not complete |  
| Implementation Overdue | Approval received but implementation target date has passed |  
| Implemented | Approved change has been implemented |  
| Archived | Submission lifecycle is complete and final records are archived |

\#\# Agency Query Assumptions

Agency queries are simulated based on realistic regulatory review themes, including:

\- CMC clarifications  
\- GMP documentation  
\- Stability data  
\- Impurity control  
\- Specifications  
\- Process validation  
\- Labelling updates  
\- Administrative clarification

Query rounds are assumed to include:

\- Validation  
\- Round 1  
\- Round 2

Response timing assumptions vary by query round and complexity.

\#\# CTD Assumptions

The content plan uses CTD-style modules:

| CTD Module | Purpose |  
|---|---|  
| Module 1 | Administrative and regional information |  
| Module 2 | Summaries and overviews |  
| Module 3 | Quality / CMC documentation |  
| Module 5 | Clinical data, where applicable |

For CMC-heavy variations, Module 3 is expected to dominate document requirements and agency queries.

\#\# Publishing Assumptions

Publishing tracker records simulate eCTD readiness and submission package status.

Publishing status may include:

\- Not Started  
\- Publishing In Progress  
\- QC Review  
\- Submitted to Health Authority  
\- Completed

Validation status may include:

\- Not Started  
\- Pending  
\- Passed  
\- Passed with Minor Comments

\#\# Archive Assumptions

Archive tracker records simulate the final regulatory archive process after submission and approval.

Archive status may include:

\- Archived  
\- Ready for Archive  
\- Not Ready \- Under Assessment  
\- Not Ready \- In Preparation  
\- Not Ready \- Not Started

Archive QC is used to identify whether the final dossier, approval letter, gateway acknowledgement, validation report, and agency correspondence are complete.

\#\# Disclaimer

This project is a fictional simulation and does not represent real EMA data, real Veeva Vault configuration, or real company regulatory records.
